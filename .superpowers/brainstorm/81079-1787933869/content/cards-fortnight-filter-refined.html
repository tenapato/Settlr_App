01 · VISIBLE CHOICES

No hidden filter menu

Previous, current, and next periods remain one tap away. The user can compare their date ranges before changing the data.

02 · DATE-FIRST COPY

Relative and exact labels work together

“Current fortnight” provides orientation; “16–31 Aug” provides certainty. At a month boundary, Next clearly reads “1–15 Sep.”

03 · ALL CARDS

The exception stays separate

All cards is not a fortnight, so it does not compete as a fourth equal segment. Selecting it switches the summary to the chosen statement month and visibly activates the capsule.

04 · INTERACTION

Selection follows the finger

Tap or horizontal swipe advances one period with a light selection haptic. Content cross-fades; the cards do not slide as if changing tabs.

Existing behavior retained: cross-month card merging, resolved statement-month writes, previous/current/next/all states, empty-state fallback, and the current server endpoints.
