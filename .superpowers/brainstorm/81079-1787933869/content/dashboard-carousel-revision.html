01 · POSITION

After the monthly summary

The user reads the available balance and income/spend context first. The moving insights then add interpretation without competing with the primary number.

02 · BEHAVIOR

Existing mechanics preserved

Seamless 30pt/sec loop, touch pause, one-to-one drag, 2.5-second idle resume, and tap-through to category detail remain unchanged.

03 · ACCESSIBILITY

Motion remains optional

Reduce Motion replaces auto-scroll with the current static horizontal row. VoiceOver reads the combined insight summary and exposes the drill-through action.

04 · RESTYLE ONLY

No surrounding card

The ticker stays plain text on a subtle structural band. No glow, gradient, or additional carousel indicators are introduced.

Locked for the final spec: `SpendingInsights.build`, ranking, maximum insight count, drag/tap behavior, resume timing, and Reduce Motion fallback are preserved. Only theme tokens, placement, typography, and surrounding spacing change.
