Design section 03 · Core tab roots

Each screen answers one financial question first.

The roots share a visual grammar but not a card template. Information density changes by task: glanceable on Home, searchable in Activity, progress-led in Savings, and obligation-led in Cards.

Cards

The next obligation leads. Cards are ordered by due date, and payments/installments are integrated instead of living in a separate tab.

HOME PATTERN

Decision-first dashboard: current position, context, then drill-down.

ACTIVITY PATTERN

Searchable unified ledger with filters and explicit filtered totals.

SAVINGS PATTERN

Accounts as goals, with progress and automation visible together.

CARDS PATTERN

Upcoming obligation before visual card identity or historical detail.

Reference synthesis: Copilot’s decision-led dashboard and filterable transaction model, Monzo’s separate “future money” framing and goal pots, and Nubank’s obligation-first card payment flow. Settlr keeps its own data model, language, and Signal Black system.
