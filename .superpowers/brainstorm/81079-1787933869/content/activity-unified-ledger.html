The expense appears once in the ledger with a Split marker. Tapping it opens transaction detail with a route into the associated split.

02 · ATTENTION LANE

Open splits stay actionable

Pending claims and queued offline work sit above the ledger. They disappear from this module after completion instead of creating a second history row.

Type chips cover everyday scanning. The circular filter button opens a native sheet for category, card, verified state, split status, and custom date range.

04 · NAVIGATION

Rows open the right depth

Transactions use a detail sheet for quick inspection and editing. Active splits push into their collaborative flow so back reliably returns to Activity.
