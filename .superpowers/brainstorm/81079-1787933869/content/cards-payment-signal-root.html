01 · PRIMARY QUESTION

What must I pay soon?

The first number spans the upcoming two-week window. Users see urgency before utilization, transaction history, or card configuration.

02 · CARD RAIL

Exposure is spatial

A horizontal rail keeps cards as peer objects. Each card shows remaining due, utilization, and date; tapping pushes into card detail.

03 · PAYMENT ACTION

The launcher stays global

Opening the C6 launcher from Cards includes Record payment as the context-aware first manual action. It returns to the same Cards position after save.

04 · STATES

Risk uses language and structure

On track, Due soon, Overdue, and Paid appear as explicit labels. Lime communicates positive/current state; overdue relies on icon, copy, and contrast—not a new accent family.

Current API binding: payment summary supplies due, recorded, utilization, and paid state; credit usage series powers detail charts; monthly payments and deferred plans stay inside card detail.
